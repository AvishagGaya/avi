# TEJÁ House · Event Agreement

Cais do Sodré 5, Lisboa · Operated by Status Terrace, Unipessoal, Lda
Bookings: curation@tejahouse.com · Extras and production: ola@tejahouse.com

Every event signs this, paid or free. It says what you get, what you don't, who does what, and what happens if plans change. Read it before you pay anything. It has no prices. Prices live in the Rate Sheet, which we send when you ask.

A booking is confirmed when this agreement is signed by both sides and the deposit (if there is one) has arrived. A deposit without a signature isn't a booking. A signature without a deposit holds the date for 24 hours.

## 2. What the space fee covers

**The space fee is for the venue. Only the venue.**

Included:
- Use of the rooms named above, for the hours named above
- The furniture in those rooms as it stands
- Toilets, house lighting, heating or cooling, Wi-Fi
- A house contact on the night

Not included. Each of these is an extra, priced separately and agreed in writing:
- Sound system and sound engineer
- DJ equipment, microphones, projector, extra lighting
- Security
- Cleaning beyond normal use
- Host, door staff, bar staff
- Food and drink
- Decor, production, photography, filming
- Hours beyond those booked
- Music licences, permits and insurance for your event

If it isn't listed as included, it's an extra.

## 3. Who does what

**Our responsibility**
- Hand over the space clean, safe and open at the agreed time
- Keep the building licensed, with working fire exits and toilets
- Run the house bar and kitchen
- Coordinate every extra we have agreed with you in writing
- Tell you straight away about any change on our side

**Your responsibility**
- Pay on the dates in section 6
- Keep to the guest maximum and the hours booked
- Your event, your guests, your guest list, your ticketing
- Your performers, suppliers and anyone you bring in
- Any damage caused by you, your guests or your suppliers
- Load in and out inside the booked hours, and leave the space as you found it
- Music rights, permits and insurance for your event, unless we confirm in writing that the house covers them
- Follow our team and our security on the night

## 4. Extras and production

Extras are coordinated with **ola@tejahouse.com**. Write there with what you need. We reply with a price. An extra isn't booked until you've agreed the price in writing. Anything you add on the night is charged at the Rate Sheet price.

**Sound.** The house sound system is an extra unless the box in section 1 says included.

**Sound engineer.** Any event with DJ equipment or live music on the house system has our sound engineer on, as an extra. He runs the system and keeps the sound inside the limits of the building. If you want your own engineer, ask first. We say yes in writing, and your engineer follows our house rules and our limiter. Without our approval, nobody else touches the house system.

**Security.** Required for any event that runs past midnight, and for any event above 100 guests. We arrange it. The package is two guards for six hours. Each extra hour is €50. Security holds your guest list at the door and can refuse entry.

## 5. Kitchen, bar and closing a floor

- The bar and the kitchen are ours. **Bar revenue stays with the house and isn't split**, unless the special terms in section 10 say otherwise in writing.
- All food and drink is served by the house. No outside food or drink. Outside vendors only with our written approval.
- The restaurant keeps trading in any room you haven't closed.
- **If you close a floor or room the restaurant would normally use, you guarantee a minimum spend** at the bar and kitchen, set in section 10. If your guests spend less, you pay the difference. We invoice it after the event.

## 6. Money

- All prices exclude IVA at 23%.
- 50% of the space fee holds the date. The balance is due 7 days before the event.
- Extras are invoiced in the same schedule, or after the event when they depend on the night (extra hours, extra security).
- A refundable damage deposit may apply. It's listed on your quote. We return it within 7 days if nothing is damaged.
- Late payment: we can release the date.

## 7. Cancellation and changes

**If you cancel:**
- 30 days or more before: we return the deposit, or move it to a new date within 6 months.
- 14 to 29 days before: we keep the deposit.
- Less than 14 days before, or no-show: the full space fee is due, plus the cost of any extras we've already booked (security, engineer, staff), plus the minimum spend if you closed a floor.

**Free events and partnerships.** No fee doesn't mean no cost. If you cancel inside 14 days, you pay what we've committed for your event: staff, security, and the minimum spend for any floor we closed.

**If we cancel**, or the weather or a safety order closes the building, you get a full refund or a new date. That's all we owe.

**Changing the date** needs our written agreement and depends on availability. Without it, it counts as a cancellation.

## 8. House rules

- Guest maximum is the legal capacity for the room. Any increase needs our written approval.
- Music and doors follow the times in section 1. Overruns are charged per hour.
- Cups stay inside after 23:00. No glass outside.
- Nothing fixed to walls, ceilings or floors. Nothing moved out of a room without putting it back.
- No smoking inside. No illegal substances. Our team and security can stop the event if safety or the law is at risk.
- Guest list at the door when security is on. Only registered guests enter.
- Commercial photo or film shoots need approval.
- We photograph the space at handover and at the end. Damage is judged against those photos.

## 9. Liability

You're responsible for your guests, your suppliers and your equipment while you're in the space. We aren't responsible for belongings left in the venue. We recommend event insurance. This agreement is under Portuguese law. Lisbon courts decide any dispute.

## Annex · Partnership and free events

Applies when the booking type in section 1 is C or D.

- No space fee, or a reduced one, as written in section 1.
- You keep your ticket revenue unless section 10 says otherwise.
- The house keeps the bar. A minimum spend or bar guarantee may apply, as in section 5.
- Security, sound engineer, cleaning and door staff are extras unless section 10 says they're covered.
- Both sides approve names, artwork and promotion that use the TEJÁ name before they go out.
- Cancelling for no reason is covered by section 7. It applies even if no money has changed hands.
