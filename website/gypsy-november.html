gypsy, november

Our kitchen is Gypsy, and in november it sets six tables: a launch for the autumn menu, the last dinner of the Mushroom Festival, two lunches, one evening eaten blindfolded and one dinner moved into the sound room.

Every one of them is served family style, with the dishes in the middle and the seating shared, and every one of them is listed here as it is confirmed.

Our dishes will be served family style, made for sharing, filling the table for everyone to enjoy together. Just as we share the food, we hope to share stories, conversations, and good moments around the same table.
