## 1. Overview

Welcome, Mayan! TEJÁ House has three spaces, each with its own personality, noise level, and kind of event. Your job is to match every inquiry to the right space, apply the right rules and price, and keep the house running smoothly for guests, neighbours, and the team.

**The golden rules (memorize these five):**

1. **Minimum booking: 2 hours**, plus a **1-hour buffer before and after** every event.
2. **Noise:** no loud sound before 8, except on **Sundays** (the neighbours are closed).
3. **Parties finish by 6 am** and **require security (€300)**.
4. **Restaurant hours (12–3 and 3–6)** shape what can happen in the Main Hall.
5. **When a general rule and a specific exception clash, the exception wins** (e.g. Sunday loud sound).

## 3. Booking Guidelines

**Time rules.** Every booking is **at least 2 hours**, because switching the space over is hard. Every event also gets a **1-hour buffer before and after**, so the calendar is never too tight. When you check availability, block the buffers too (a 10 pm to 2 am event holds 9 pm to 3 am).

**Parties.** Parties should finish **around 6 am at the latest**, because we reopen the next day and need time to clean. Parties **always include security (€300)**. Minimum party booking is **6 hours**. With the closing buffer, a 6 am finish means the space is held until 7 am.

> **Original wording, verbatim:**
>
> * I think we want to have a minimum booking of 2 hours because doing switches is hard, and a 1-hour booking before or after each event, so it's not too tight.
> * For parties, we want to finish them more or less around 6 in the morning because then we need to reopen the next day and have cleaning. Parties also need to have security.
> * I think we already have a lot of these rules.

**Quick check before saying "yes":**

- [ ] Does the event fit the space's noise level and the restaurant's hours?
- [ ] Is it at least 2 hours, with 1-hour buffers free on both sides?
- [ ] Is it a party? If so: at least 6 hours, ends by 6 am, security (€300) included.
- [ ] Is any loud sound planned before 8? Only OK in the Sound Room on a Sunday.
- [ ] Does it need a sound engineer or equipment? (See Live Music below.)

## 5. Onboarding Workflow

### A. Answering an inquiry

1. **Acknowledge fast.** Reply within `[FILL: response time]` using the email template below.
2. **Collect the basics** with the inquiry form (Section 6): date, time, type of event, headcount, sound needs, who is organizing.
3. **Match the space** using the table in Section 2. Redirect politely if the idea doesn't fit (e.g. amplified music in the Main Hall during restaurant hours).
4. **Check the calendar** for the requested slot plus 1-hour buffers on both sides.
5. **Apply the rules** from the Section 3 checklist.
6. **Quote** using the right package and budget build-up (Section 4). Quote only numbers that appear in this document.
7. **Confirm and hold** the date, and log it at `[FILL: booking calendar / tool]`.

### B. Managing the open call

1. **Publish the call** on `[FILL: channels]` with the date window and event types we welcome (free community gatherings, networking, jazz, jam sessions, vinyl nights).
2. **Collect submissions** through the inquiry form.
3. **Screen** each one: does it fit a space, the noise rules, and the restaurant hours? Free community and networking events belong in the Main Hall.
4. **Shortlist** and send to `[FILL: decision-maker]` for sign-off.
5. **Reply to everyone**: accepted (with next steps), or a kind "not this time" with an alternative if there is one.
6. **Schedule accepted events** with their buffers and log them.

### C. Applying the budgeting framework

1. Identify the **category** (live music, nightlife party, dinner).
2. Pick the **package** (own curation or external/corporate).
3. Start from the **package minimum**, then add **security (€300)**, **cleaning**, **staff**, and **lights/sound rental**.
4. Check the booking length (2 h minimum, 6 h for parties) and add the buffers to the calendar.
5. Send the quote and note what's included.

### D. When in doubt

Ask `[FILL: contact email]` before promising anything. A slow "yes" beats a wrong "yes."
